The story so far

From sketchbooksto systems.

Every design starts as an empty frame. Mine started with a question: what do I want to create?

I am not the user. It is the rule I still design by.

  1. 01

    The spark

    I grew up around colors. My mom is an art teacher, so sketching was never just a hobby at home. I knew I loved creating long before I knew what to create.

  2. 02

    Learning the basics

    A designer I knew told me design was a real path, and my brother showed me where the world was heading: screens, apps, interfaces. I learned from YouTube, college and courses. My first practice project was a Google Calendar redesign.

  3. 03

    The wrong frame

    I once designed a full set of mobile screens inside a desktop frame and had to redo everything. Now I check every spec before I start.

  4. 04

    First real client

    A friend started a clothing brand, and I designed his complete brand identity and website. It taught me that design is about what the brand needs to say and what its customers need to feel.

  5. 05

    Leveling up

    From screens to systems: service design, product design, CMF, and an international Demola project with a global team working on real problems.

  6. 06

    The hardest level

    Letting go of ideas I love when users prove them wrong. Testing taught me that a design isn’t finished when I like it. It’s finished when it works for the people using it.

04Projects

  1. 2026Demola team project

    Test Mode AI Usability Testing Agent for Figma

    • Co-designed an AI agent built into Figma that tests a single feature against the project brief (KPIs, stakeholders) and the design system, then routes each issue to the responsible team
    • Narrowed 21 testing methods down to 6 that fit post-design, feature-level testing: accessibility, design-system consistency, content/microcopy review, heuristic evaluation, cognitive walkthrough, and brief/KPI alignment
    • Designed synthetic stakeholders — AI personas built from the brief — that run every check and deliver one consolidated result
  2. 2026Self-initiated project

    Continuity HR Employee Handover Platform

    • Identified a real HR problem — lost institutional knowledge during employee transitions — backed by industry data (42% of company knowledge held by individuals; $47M average annual productivity loss)
    • Ran competitor analysis against BambooHR and Sensay to define the product gap and built a Jobs-to-be-Done framework across HR, Employee, and Supervisor roles
    • Designed an end-to-end HR dashboard, AI-suggested task workflows, a three-stage approval process, and an employee self-service portal
    • Mapped a Value Proposition Canvas connecting user pain points and gains directly to product features
  3. 2 weeksService design case study

    RC Renewal RTO Service Redesign

    • Conducted 10+ user interviews to map the offline RC renewal journey and surface key pain points through service blueprinting
    • Created user personas, empathy maps, and an as-is vs. to-be service blueprint comparing offline and digital renewal flows
    • Designed a digital-first solution with OCR/DigiLocker document upload, real-time status tracking, and a digital RC wallet
    • Validated the design through usability testing with 5 participants, achieving 40% faster task completion than the existing RTO portal
  4. 2025Heuristic evaluation

    Parivahan Sewa Redesign

    • Performed a heuristic evaluation of the government Parivahan Sewa portal’s Learner’s Licence flow against established usability principles
    • Identified issues in visual consistency, information hierarchy, and system-to-real-world match, and proposed redesign recommendations
  5. 2026Collaborative project

    UX for Emerging Technologies Dementia Care

    • Researched the health & security domain, focusing on dementia care for patients, caregivers, and families
    • Explored technology-driven approaches to improve patient safety and support caregiver wellbeing
  6. 2025UX/UI case study

    AI Health Care Tracking Mobile App

    • Designed an AI-powered health companion app for elderly users and people with chronic conditions, combining wearable integration, voice interaction, and smart analytics
    • Conducted qualitative research (interviews, focus groups) and a quantitative survey using purposive sampling to uncover real needs around medication adherence and emergency response
    • Ran secondary research and competitor analysis (Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, Samsung Health) to identify a gap in AI-driven insights for elderly and chronic-illness care
    • Defined UX priorities — voice-first simplified interfaces, unified health summaries, and transparent privacy policies — to drive the design direction
